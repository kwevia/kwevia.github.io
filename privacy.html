Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Kwevia AutoPost ("the tool", "we") handles. The tool is a private publishing tool that Kwevia uses to post its own content to its own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

  • Facebook, Instagram and YouTube: access tokens for the Kwevia Page and channels, used only to publish Kwevia's own posts and first comments.
  • Content: the videos, images and captions Kwevia creates for posting.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect information about viewers, followers or any other Meta or Google users. We do not read messages, contacts, analytics of other accounts, or any data beyond what is listed above.

How information is stored

Access tokens and content are stored only on the account owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by Kwevia. Information is sent only to the platform being posted to (Meta or Google), through their official APIs.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Content is shared only with the platform it is published to.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the tool. You can revoke access at any time:

  • Meta: Facebook Settings → Business integrations → remove Kwevia AutoPost.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove Kwevia AutoPost.

Removing access, or deleting the tool's local files, deletes all stored tokens. To ask about or request deletion of any information, email us.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is used only by the Kwevia account owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
